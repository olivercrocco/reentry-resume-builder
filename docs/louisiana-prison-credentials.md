# Programs and credentials available in Louisiana prisons

Compiled 13 November 2026 to feed the resume builder's certification, education, and work-history lists. Everything below comes from the sources listed at the end. Where I filled in a certifying body that a source does not name, it is marked "(added)".

## The main source

The Louisiana Department of Public Safety and Corrections publishes a Catalog of Rehabilitative Services. The 2024-2025 edition (December 2024, 66 pages) lists the career and technical education (CTE) programs on pages 11 and 12, with descriptions on pages 13 to 21. It says which facilities offer each program in "Attachment B State Facilities / Attachment C Local Facilities," but those attachments were not in the downloaded PDF, so the facility-by-facility breakdown below is partial.

An undated department presentation, posted by the state education department, adds scale: education programs in 33 of 64 parishes, 5,244 CTE and industry-based certification enrollments and 2,959 completions, 135 separate stackable credentials from 18 agencies across 42 craft areas, and about 10,000 completers over five years. The partner colleges are Fletcher, Northshore Technical, Central Louisiana Technical, Louisiana Delta, South Louisiana, and Bossier Parish community colleges (all LCTCS).

## Certifying bodies named in the catalog

- NCCER (National Center for Construction Education and Research)
- ASE (Automotive Service Excellence), with NATEF-accredited automotive programs (except at Dixon)
- I-CAR (collision repair)
- EETC (outdoor power equipment)
- HVAC Excellence and EPA (HVAC); the usual EPA credential for refrigerant work is Section 608 (added)
- AWS (American Welding Society): students are prepared to take the AWS exam
- National Restaurant Association: ServSafe Manager
- IC3 Digital Literacy, Levels 1 to 3 (issued by Certiport, added)
- Microsoft Office Specialist: Word, PowerPoint, Excel
- Certified Logistics Associate and Certified Logistics Technician (MSSC, added)
- Electrical Generating Systems Association (EGSA): generator repair
- OSHA 10-Hour and 30-Hour
- iCEV certifications: Express Employment Professionals (Business Office Technology; Career Preparedness), American Meat Science Association (Culinary Meat Selection and Cookery; Food Safety and Science; Meat Evaluation), Center for Financial Responsibility (Personal Financial Literacy), BASF (Plant Science), Southwest Airlines (Professional Communications), SFMA (Turfgrass Science)
- Louisiana State Board of Cosmetology (licensing exam)
- Louisiana state horticulture exam (licensed horticulturist)

## CTE programs listed in the catalog

Automotive Technology; Braille; Building Technology; Cabling Technology (C-Tech); CPR/First Aid; Carpentry; Certified Logistics Associate; Certified Logistics Technician; Collision Repair; Concrete Finishing; Construction Management Academy; Construction Safety Technology; Core NCCER; Cosmetology; Crew Leadership; Culinary Arts; Ditch Witch Operator; Drywall; Electrical; Electronic Systems Technology; Fall Protection; Fiber Optics (Technician, Premise Cabling Technician, Splicing Specialist, Testing Specialist); Field Safety; Forklift Operation; Furniture Manufacturing and Restoration; Generator Repair; Heating/Ventilation/Air-Conditioning/Refrigeration; Heavy Equipment Operation; Horticulture; IC3 Digital Literacy; iCEV Certifications; Instructor Certification Training Program (NCCER); Introduction to Maritime; Job Life Skills; Managing Electrical Hazards; Maritime Pipefitting; Maritime Structural Fitter; Masonry; Medical Lab Assistant; Microsoft Office Specialist; Outdoor Power Equipment Technology; OSHA; Painting; Pipefitting; Pipeline Technician; Plumbing; Project Management; Project Supervision; Python Computer Coding; Rigger; Safety Technology; Signal Person; Site Layout; ServSafe; Truck Driving / CDL; Utility Line Locator; Welding.

Notes worth knowing when helping someone describe these:
- Truck Driving / CDL is offered only at Angola, because that is where the driving course is. The catalog calls it an overview with simulator training, so the resume should say "CDL training" unless the person actually holds a license.
- Heavy equipment training inside is "Knowledge-Based" only, since the equipment cannot be operated on prison grounds (2021 catalog).
- Utility Line Locator is limited to Acadiana-region facilities and ends with an NCCER Core designation.
- Welding covers oxy-fuel cutting, flux-cored and gas metal arc welding, pipe welding, plasma arc cutting, and blueprint reading.

## Louisiana State Penitentiary (Angola) specifically

From the department's page for the penitentiary:
- Education: New Orleans Baptist Theological Seminary; Ashland University (bachelor's in general studies); Upper Iowa University; ABE, GED, and HiSET; literacy; Pre-Release Exit Program (PREP).
- Vocational: carpentry, culinary arts, horticulture, welding.
- Reentry and rehabilitative: Automotive Technology (ASE); Building and General Construction; Collision Repair (ASE); Corrections Reentry Court Workforce Program; Electrician; HVAC Excellence certification; Outdoor Power Equipment; Print Ed certification; NCCER certification in carpentry, welding, heating and air conditioning, electrical, industrial painting, concrete finishing, and brick masonry; eyeglass repair.

## College and degree programs

- New Orleans Baptist Theological Seminary, at Angola and the Louisiana Correctional Institute for Women: associate, bachelor's, and master's degrees in theology. Many graduates serve as mentors and peer ministers, some at other prisons under the chaplain.
- Ashland University (Ohio): associate and bachelor's degrees by tablet in more than a dozen state and local facilities, since 2016.
- Loyola University New Orleans, Tulane University, and Fletcher Technical Community College: associate, bachelor's, and technical diploma programs.
- LCTCS colleges: college credit plus industry-based certifications.

## Prison Enterprises (state prison industries)

- Agriculture at Angola: corn and soybeans on about 3,000 acres, and a cow-calf operation of about 2,000 head. Cattle also at Elayn Hunt, Dixon (heifer development), and David Wade (calf backgrounding).
- Products: garments and uniforms; janitorial products (cleaners, mops, brooms); metal fabrication (bed frames, lockers, barricades, trash receptacles); seating; wooden furniture; bedding, mattresses, and linens; silk screening and laser engraving (signs, decals, plaques).
- Services: graphics and printing; custom embroidery; license plate manufacturing (the "Tag Plant"); furniture restoration; janitorial services and groundskeeping for state office buildings in Baton Rouge; the canteen package program.

## Clubs and roles that can go under volunteer and leadership

From the catalog's clubs and organizations section (mostly Angola): Toastmasters (Gavel Club); CPR T.E.A.M., which has trained or recertified more than 6,000 people including staff and first responders; Compassionate Care, trained volunteers who assist gravely or terminally ill patients; the Hospice club; H.E.L.P.E.R. peer health educators; L.E.T.S. M.O.V.E. mentoring for men 25 and under; certified mentors in the Corrections Reentry Court Workforce Program; Civic Leadership Association; Jaycees; Human Relations and Civic Organization; I.R.O.C. sports officials; Visiting Concession Club (inventory, ordering, sales reports); Veterans Incarcerated; the Music Association; the Toy Shop; and prison magazines (The Angolite at Angola, Walk Talk at Elayn Hunt, Straight Low at Dixon).

## Gaps to close with staff

- Which programs each client actually completed, and whether they have their certificates or cards. NCCER credentials can be looked up in NCCER's registry, which helps when paperwork is lost.
- The facility attachments (B and C) that the catalog refers to.
- Program names change, so check against what clients remember.
